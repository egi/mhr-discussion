# Notice

## Status

This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan and educational project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the publisher of the game.

## Intellectual property

The publisher owns the game, its characters, card artwork and rules. This repository contains no rulebook and no card artwork.

## Licence scope

The MIT licence in [LICENSE](./LICENSE) covers code only.

The header comment of each of the 43 scripted cards contains a short quote of that card's printed effect text. That quote is the publisher's text. It is included for contributor readability only and is excluded from the MIT licence. No other card text is included in this repository, and contributions must not add any.

## Takedown

Takedown requests are honoured. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for the contact and the process. Quotes are removed within 72 hours of a request, and each removal is noted in [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md).

Contributions are licensed under the repository licence. There is no CLA and no DCO.
