# 02 - BP01-001: do attached cards of the retreated characters count toward X?

**Status: RESOLVED (rulebook and card wording; strong but not an explicit sentence)**

> Source note: L<n> citations refer to a private chat export and cannot be re-checked by readers. This text is a paraphrase, and people appear by role only.

## The question

BP01-001 is called by retreating characters, and its trigger prunes an opponent character of Lv X or below, where X is the number of cards retreated by calling it. Suppose a character with attached cards is retreated for the calling cost, and its attached cards go to the retreat zone together with it. Does X count only the host, or the host plus its attached cards? The answer changes how large a prune is possible.

## What the head judge said

Nothing on this exact case. The head judge was tagged on it at least twice (L3699, L18871) and did not reply; around the time of the October debate the head judge was away (L19036). Adjacent statements, for context only: an attached card is a card with its own identity and its effect refers to itself, not to its host (L12510-12511); attachments return to hand with a host that returns to hand (L16418-16442). Neither says whether a following attachment counts as retreated for an effect.

## Verdict

Authority tier: RULEBOOK and CARD-TEXT. X counts the characters and face-down cards used as the calling cost only. Attached cards do not add to it. Calling a Lv4+ character requires retreating characters whose combined Lv equals the called Lv, and face-down cards may stand in as Lv1 each. The card design uses two distinct phrasings: effects that say "by calling" (BP01-026, SD01-018, BP01-001) and attachment cards that say they follow their host into retreat (SP01-014, SP01-024, SP01-028, SP01-037). The two are deliberately different events, so an attachment that follows its host is not retreated by calling.

The remaining counter-argument is that no sentence in the rulebook says outright that attachments do not count; it is outweighed by the structure above rather than refuted. A question for the head judge would make it airtight.

## Rulebook check

- 301.19.b: the calling cost for a Lv4 or higher character is retreating characters on the field whose combined Lv matches.
- 301.21.g: face-down cards may be retreated toward the cost, each treated as one Lv1 card.
- 301.25.c: an attached card follows the card it is attached to; the host does not follow its attachment.
- 301.25.l: attached cards go to the retreat zone when the host stops being a character.
- 301.16.a.4: an attached card retreating is resolved as its own retreat of a card.
