# 07 - SP01-061: does its flip reach enemy-owned cards sitting in my base?

**Status: RESOLVED (card text and rulebook; the flip includes enemy-owned cards in your base)**

> Source note: L<n> citations refer to a private chat export and cannot be re-checked by readers. This text is a paraphrase, and people appear by role only.

## The question

Some effects put an opponent's cards into your base (for example SP01-042 and a hand-to-base effect of another character). SP01-061 (green, Lv6) has an activated effect from the hand that flips all your cards in BASE. Are enemy-owned cards in your base flipped too, and where do they go afterwards? A second question: the English and Indonesian wording of this card differ, and which one counts.

## What the head judge said

Nothing on this exact question. Adjacent statements:

- 17 Sep (L5304-5312): on a different card that says "your cards in base", an enemy card sent into your field counts as yours, because the controller decides.
- 25 Sep (L11011): he said he was checking the wording of SP01-061.
- 29 Sep (L12971-12972, L13013-13014): when members said the English and Indonesian text differ, the English one is right. A flip opens the closed and closes the open; a cover only closes.
- 1 Oct (L14670): a flip does not count as entering the field.

He did not answer the original question that morning.

## Verdict

Authority tier: RULEBOOK and CARD-TEXT, with the head judge's English-wins ruling (HEAD-JUDGE) on the wording. The English text decides, not the Indonesian one. It counts set cards and characters in the base, and says "flip". It speaks of "your" cards in BASE, not cards whose owner is you, and the rulebook makes the controller the player whose field area holds the card, so enemy-owned cards in your base are flipped (closed ones open, open ones close). They stay under your control while there. If they leave the field they go to their owner's zones. Whether a flipped card may battle-base move at once, and what happens to attachments, were answered only by single members and are not part of this ruling.

## Rulebook check

- 301.4.a: the controller of a card is the player whose FIELD area contains it.
- 301.3.e: a card that retreats goes to its owner's RETREAT.
- 301.22.b: a character changed back from face-down is not subject to the new-card movement restriction.
- 301.22.c: face-down cards in BASE are turned up by a flip effect.
- 301.22.d: if the unique ability would block it, the card is revealed instead of flipped.
- 301.46: "All" means the maximum possible selection; 301.2.b: several cards are processed one at a time.

## Scripts and engine

SP01-061 is not scripted. SP01-042 (owner-based wording) is also not scripted. Scripts: none known.
