# 06 - BP01-033: does a retarget trigger "when your character attacks" again?

**Status: ESCALATE (rulebook silent; question open for the head judge)**

> Source note: L<n> citations refer to a private chat export and cannot be re-checked by readers. This text is a paraphrase, and people appear by role only.

## The question

BP01-033 (yellow, Lv4, in BACK) triggers when one of your Avengers characters attacks, and gives one opposing battle character a Power reduction for the turn. A member described a case where the defender counter-calls and the attacker retargets. Does the retarget fire the "attacks" trigger a second time?

## Positions

Two members, one each way. The thread moved on.

## What the head judge said

Nothing about this card or case. Related statements:

- 24 Sep (L9955-9961): an "attacks" trigger cannot be played after both players pass and the attack resolves, because the trigger runs when the attack is declared.
- 6 Oct (L17571-17572): not attacking means never declaring; failing to attack means declared but failed. An attack is declared once, and a failed one still counts as declared.

## Verdict

Authority tier: none; this is ESCALATE and stays open. The question is not settled. The rulebook is silent on whether returning to the Select Target Step fires "attacks" triggers again. Two readings are available and neither is established:

- Fires once: an attack is declared once (303.2.a.4.4, 303.2.a.4.5, 301.50), and the head judge's declare-time principle points that way.
- Fires again: "attacks" triggers are resolved in the Select Target Step (303.2.a.4.3.1.1, 303.2.a.4.3.1.2), and a retarget returns to that very step to choose a target again (303.2.a.4.3.2.2).

One earlier member belief, that a retarget always opens a new counter window, predates the 24 Sep update; discussion 05 shows the rulebook does lead to a new counter step after reselection, but that does not by itself create a new declaration. Suggested question for the head judge: if an attack goes back to the Select Target Step and chooses a new target, does "When your character attacks" trigger again?

## Rulebook check

- 303.2.a.4.3.1.1: in the Select Target Step a character attacks by choosing a target within its range.
- 303.2.a.4.3.1.2: effects triggered in that step resolve after the choice.
- 303.2.a.4.3.2.2: an invalid target sends the battle back to the Select Target Step.
- 303.2.a.4.4: each character has one attack opportunity per turn; 303.2.a.4.5: once an attack is declared, the battle process is locked in.
- 301.50: "Has Attacked" status.
