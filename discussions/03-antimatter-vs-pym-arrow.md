# 03 - BP01-001 versus BP01-099: does the trigger still work if its card was just pruned?

**Status: RESOLVED (rulebook and card text; both triggers resolve, turn player first)**

> Source note: L<n> citations refer to a private chat export and cannot be re-checked by readers. This text is a paraphrase, and people appear by role only.

## The question

An opponent calls BP01-001 (Lv6) and its trigger prunes my BP01-099. BP01-099 triggers when an opponent character of Lv4 or above enters. Can its trigger still resolve and prune the BP01-001 even though BP01-099 has just been pruned? Members called this one of the most frequently asked questions.

Eight members and trusted judges gave the both-resolve answer; one member dissented once and later accepted the other view.

## What the head judge said

Nothing on this exact pair. Three statements of principle all fit the both-resolve answer, provided the discard is the only cost:

- 20 Sep (L6489-6491): there will be no FAQ entry, one will be added later, and understanding "do as much as you can" resolves many such questions.
- 25 Sep, after the update (L10998-10999): replying to a member who thought the new rules changed this, said it is still "do as much as you can" (a general resolution principle, on a different card).
- 2 Oct (L15380): on simultaneous triggers for a different pair, effects resolve one at a time and a later one cannot resolve if its condition is no longer met.

## Verdict

Authority tier: RULEBOOK and CARD-TEXT. Both triggers resolve. The turn player's trigger (BP01-001) resolves first and prunes BP01-099. BP01-099's already-triggered effect then resolves: its discard cost is paid, it prunes what it still can (itself is already gone) and prunes the BP01-001. The card text for BP01-099 puts only the discard before its "if you do", so pruning itself is part of the effect. Whether the 24 Sep update changed trigger timing was raised, but every post-update statement still says both run.

## Rulebook check

- 304.1.c: a trigger resolves immediately when it triggers.
- 304.1.d: an effect must be resolved to the greatest extent possible once it starts.
- 304.1.e: while one triggered effect resolves, the others already triggered stay pending until it finishes.
- 304.1.g: when both players trigger effects at the same timing, the turn player orders theirs first.

## Scripts and engine

Neither BP01-001 nor BP01-099 is scripted. Scripts: none known.
