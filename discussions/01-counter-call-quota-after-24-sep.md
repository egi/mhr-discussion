# 01 - Counter-call quota after the 24 Sep 2026 rules update

**Status: RESOLVED (rulebook: one counter call per battle phase and one per counter phase, for each player)**

> Source note: L<n> citations refer to a private chat export and cannot be re-checked by readers. This text is a paraphrase, and people appear by role only.

## The question

After the 24 Sep 2026 update, how many counter calls (calling a character from hand during a counter step or counter phase) may a player make? Three readings appeared: one per turn in total, one per battle phase plus one per counter phase, or one per counter step.

## Positions

|---|---|---|---|
| member | 11 Sep | L494-497 | Before the update: one in the battle phase counter steps and one in the counter phase, two per turn. A second member agreed. |
| trusted judge | 16 Sep, 20 Sep | L5176, L6279 | Before the update: one per battle phase however many counter steps it has, plus one per counter phase. |

| head judge | 24 Sep | L9848 | Said only that the wording was being corrected to avoid confusion; no number was stated. |
| trusted judge | 24 Sep | L10619 | Re-posted the announcement recap in the corrected form: the counter phase still allows a call; the per-step wording in that recap was loose. |
| trusted judge | 26 Sep | L12060-12069 | One per battle phase and one per counter phase; the quota is not refreshed until the next turn. Gave a worked example with a full battle zone. |
| trusted judge | 26 Sep | L12223-12226 | Many counter steps may occur in one battle phase but only one call among them; the call goes to an empty slot only, battle or base. |
| member, trusted judge | 1 Oct | L14294-14302 | A member asked whether the counter call was unchanged; a trusted judge confirmed, saying what changed was the counter step and phase procedure. |
| member, trusted judge | 9 Oct | L19045-19047 | Asked for the count per battle phase: one. A free placement by a card effect does not use up the quota. |

Roughly seven members and trusted judges stated the one-plus-one reading after the correction; one member held the one-per-turn reading at the moment of the announcement and no later statement repeated it. Nobody stated the per-step reading as a position.

## What the head judge said

Only that the wording of the announcement was being corrected (L9848). No quota number was given in the chat, and no later head judge message contradicted the one-plus-one answers that followed.

## Verdict

Authority tier: RULEBOOK. The limit is one counter call in the battle phase (all its counter steps together) and one in the counter phase, and it applies to each player separately. This agrees with the trusted-judge answers. The per-step wording in the recap was a loose paraphrase and is wrong.

Related points, all after 24 Sep and consistent with the above: a counter call targets an empty slot only (L12225-12226); effects that place a character without calling it do not use the quota (L19047); a swap is not a counter call (L10516, L13137).

## Rulebook check

- 303.2.a.4.3.2.3.1.1: governs the counter step; each player may perform the counter call at most once per turn during that phase.
- 303.2.a.5.2.1.1: governs the counter phase with the same limit.
- 301.19.f: states that CALL is generally related to COUNTER CALL and points at the two rules above.
- 305.1.a: the counter keyword lets a card be called using a counter call.

## Scripts and engine

SP01-043 places a character by its own effect rather than by calling it, so it is the relevant script for the "placement does not use the quota" point. Other scripts: none known.
