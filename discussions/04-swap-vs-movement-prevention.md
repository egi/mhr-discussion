# 04 - Swap versus an effect that prevents moving (BP01-050)

**Status: RESOLVED (rulebook: a swap that would move a protected card cannot be resolved)**

> Source note: L<n> citations refer to a private chat export and cannot be re-checked by readers. This text is a paraphrase, and people appear by role only.

## The question

BP01-050 (yellow, Lv3) has a trigger, when it enters the field by calling, that stops one opposing character from moving for the turn. Several cards swap two cards on the field (for example BP01-061 and SD03-009). Does a swap count as "moving", so that the protected character cannot be swapped out?

## Positions

| Role | Date | Cite | Paraphrased position |
|---|---|---|---|
| member | 14 Sep | L2451-2462 | Swap keeps all state of both cards; wondered half-jokingly whether the prevention card answers swaps. Ambiguous. |
| head judge | 22 Sep | L8121-8126 | On a swap card: a swap retains every condition of the exchanged cards. This says what a swap keeps, not whether movement prevention applies. |
| member | 1 Oct | L14133-14158 | Applied the swap rule: on the field it counts as moving, from the hand it counts as placing. |
| member | 6 Oct | L17531-17554 | Read the swap rules: two field cards swapped are both treated as moving, so an effect that prevents moving also stops a field swap. |
| member | 6 Oct | L17548-17557 | Disagreed: "treated as moving" is not real moving, and plain moving differs from battle-base moving. Admitted not knowing the designers' intent. |
| members | 6 Oct | L17540-17544 | One member asked whether a swap between battle and base is a battle-base move; the answer was that it is not. |

Two members argued for blocking (one explicitly, one by implication), one against; five others had no opinion.

## What the head judge said

Nothing on this exact case. Nobody tagged the head judge on swap versus movement prevention, although the head judge was active right after the debate. The only related statement (L8121-8126) is that a swap retains all state.

## Verdict

Authority tier: RULEBOOK. A swap IS blocked by an effect that prevents moving. The rulebook has an explicit clause for this that the chat never quoted: if swapping a card would make it move, and an effect prevents that card from moving, the swap cannot be resolved. Combined with the rule that a swap of two field cards treats both as moving, the member who argued for blocking was right and the objection about "treated as" does not apply. Scope: BP01-050 protects only the character it chose and only for that turn. A swap that brings a hand card onto the field treats the hand card as placed and the field card as moving, so a protected field card still cannot be swapped out. Nothing needs to go to the head judge.

## Rulebook check

- 301.18.d: a swap of two field cards treats both as moving.
- 301.18.e to 301.18.g: the areas involved decide whether each swapped card is treated as placed or as moving; for a swap with a hand or deck card, that card is placed and the other card moves.
- 301.18.h: a swap that would move a card cannot resolve if an effect prevents that card from moving.
- 301.18.b, 301.18.c: swapped cards exchange current state and attached cards.
- 301.23 and 301.24: battle move and battle-base move are separate kinds of movement; 301.26.b: detaching is also movement; 303.2.a.4.2.1.1: rearranging is not movement.

## Scripts and engine

Scripts that touch this: BP01-050 (the movement prevention trigger), SD03-009, SD03-003 and BP01-106 (swap effects). BP01-061 is not scripted.
