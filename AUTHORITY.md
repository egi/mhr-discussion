# Authority

How rulings in `discussions/` are weighed, and what each status mark means.

## Precedence

When sources disagree, the higher entry wins. Highest first:

1. Head judge ruling. The head judge is the organiser and final authority of the community; this repository refers to that person by role only.
2. Comprehensive Rules v1.03.
3. English card text.
4. Trusted-judge consensus after 24 Sep 2026.
5. Anything else.

A chat consensus that contradicts the rulebook loses. Where a statement made before 24 Sep 2026 conflicts with one made after it, the later statement wins.

## Status marks

Every discussion carries exactly one mark on its Status line, and the status board in `discussions/00-README.md` repeats it.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| RESOLVED | Decided by the rulebook or the English card text. |
| HEAD-JUDGE | The head judge ruled on the case. |
| CONSENSUS | The head judge was silent on the exact case, trusted judges agree after 24 Sep 2026, and nothing in the rulebook contradicts them. |
| ESCALATE | Trusted judges are split, or key evidence is missing, and the head judge has not ruled. Not settled. |
| ESCALATE-OPEN | An engine or script behaviour is provisional. Not settled. |

ESCALATE and ESCALATE-OPEN items are never treated as settled. Engines, scripts and verdicts built on them must say so.

## Authority tiers in errata files

Errata and script notes name the tier a ruling came from using these labels: HEAD-JUDGE, RULEBOOK, CARD-TEXT, TRUSTED-CONSENSUS. They follow the precedence order above.

## Private source

Every `L<n>` citation in a discussion points at a line of a private chat export. That export is not published, so readers cannot re-check the line numbers. The discussions are hand-written paraphrases, people appear by role only, and nothing is quoted. The rulebook and the head judge's own rulings remain the authority; a discussion records how a conclusion was reached, it does not add to the rules.

## What is not published

The chat source is private, so anything that would identify a member of it stays private too.

- The list of trusted judges is not published.
- Rulebook text and card text are not reproduced here. Discussions cite rules by rule key and cards by card number.
- Discussion 08 is reserved and withheld until its contents are confirmed.
